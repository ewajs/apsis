# 0014. AR: the phone's own viewers

- Status: Accepted (taken unsupervised, 2026-11-28). A spacecraft in AR from step 6.1;
  the tabletop solar system in 6.2.
- Date: 2026-11-28

## Decision

- **Hand off, don't build:** a spacecraft opens in the phone's own AR viewer, Scene Viewer
  on Android and Quick Look on iPhones and iPads (ADR 0001). They place, light, scale and
  photograph better than anything we'd write, and cost us no code in the bundle.
- **The AR copy:** `npm run models` writes `public/models/ar/<id>.glb` beside each model:
  plain glTF (no Meshopt, no quantisation, no WebP: Scene Viewer's extension support isn't
  documented, plain is safe), textures at most 1024 px and JPEG unless see-through,
  **standing up** the way both viewers place a model on the floor: the antenna (+Z) up,
  its lowest point at floor level, centred. Real size. A copy over 5 MB is simplified,
  then its textures halved. Europa Clipper stays at 12.5 MB: its mesh is mostly loose
  triangles that the simplifier can't merge.
- **Android:** the card's "En tu casa (RA)" is a plain link, an `intent://` URL that
  opens Scene Viewer (`core/ar.ts`) with the AR copy's absolute URL, `ar_preferred`,
  at real size (`resizable=false`), falling back to the page.
- **iPhone:** Quick Look reads USDZ, not glTF. Rather than a second set of files, the
  AR copy is converted in the page when asked, with three's `USDZExporter`
  (`scene/ar-export.ts`, loaded then), and handed over as a blob through a link with
  `rel="ar"` around an image, as Quick Look requires. Anchored to the floor.
- **Which viewer:** Safari announces Quick Look (`relList.supports('ar')`); Android is
  recognised by its user agent; elsewhere the card offers no AR. `?ar=` forces one, for
  tests.
- **The tabletop solar system (6.2)** is made at the shown instant, so it only exists in
  the page: Quick Look takes it as a blob, but Scene Viewer, another app, can only
  fetch URLs. On Android it will be live WebXR in the page instead (6.2–6.3).

## Alternatives

- **`<model-viewer>`:** does all of this and WebXR, but bundles a second copy of three.js
  (hundreds of KB) for two links and a converter we already have.
- **USDZ files built ahead:** reliable, but they double the repository's models, and
  three's exporter needs a browser (canvas) to run.

## Moons and small bodies

In `bodies/`, loaded only when a close-up needs them (never precached). Downloaded 2026-11-28. Every map is a 2048×1024 equirectangular JPEG (quality 85) in the same
convention as the planets above: longitude 0° in the middle, east to the right (−180° at the left
edge, +180° at the right), north up. Sources that put 0° at the edges (a 0…360° domain) were rolled
by half the width. "Positive west" in a source's label only changes how longitudes are numbered,
not the picture, so nothing needed flipping. "Gaps filled" means areas the spacecraft never imaged
(no data in the source) were painted in the average colour of the imaged surface, with a soft edge.

USGS Astrogeology products are in the public domain ("USGS-authored or produced data and
information are considered to be in the U.S. Public Domain", [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits));
the licence column quotes each product page's own constraints. NASA's
[media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/): "NASA content – images,
audio, video, and media files used in the rendition of 3-dimensional models, such as texture maps and
polygon data in any format – generally are not subject to copyright in the United States."
